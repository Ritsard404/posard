# How to Manage Debts or Unpaid Orders

## What this feature does
This records sales where the customer still owes money.

## When to use it
Use this when your store allows trusted customers to pay later.

## Before you begin
- The customer should be selected or recorded.
- Your store must allow unpaid orders.
- A manager may need to approve.

## Steps
1. Create the sale in POS.
2. Choose the debt or unpaid order option when available.
3. Enter customer and due date details.
4. Record any partial payment if the customer pays some amount now.
5. Confirm the order.
6. Open **Debts** later to collect payment.

## What happens next
The sale is recorded as unpaid or partly paid. Payment collection is tracked separately.

## Tips or reminders
- Do not treat unpaid orders as cash received.
- Always choose the correct customer.

## Common questions or issues
- **The cashier cannot create debt**  
  The terminal may require manager approval or may not allow cashier debt creation.
