# How to Complete Checkout

## What this feature does
This finishes the sale and creates the receipt.

## When to use it
Use this after the cart, discount, and payment are correct.

## Before you begin
- Review all items.
- Confirm the payment amount.
- Make sure the customer is ready to finish the sale.

## Steps
1. Select **Checkout**.
2. Review the cart total, discount, and payment.
3. Confirm the sale.
4. Wait for POSard to show the completed receipt.
5. Print or share the receipt if needed.

## What happens next
The sale is saved and the receipt is ready. If the device is offline, POSard keeps the sale and updates it when connection returns.

## Tips or reminders
- Do not close the browser while the sale is finishing.
- If you are offline, keep POSard open until it can update your records later.

## Common questions or issues
- **The sale completed but did not print**  
  Check the printer and use the receipt option again.
