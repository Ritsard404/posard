# How to Manage Expenses

## What this feature does
This records store expenses such as supplies, repairs, fuel, or petty cash use.

## When to use it
Use this when the business spends money that should be included in operations reports.

## Before you begin
- You need manager access.
- Prepare the amount, category, date, and note.

## Steps
1. Open **Expenses**.
2. Add or review the expense record.
3. Choose the category.
4. Enter the amount and date.
5. Add a short note.
6. Save or submit for approval if required.

## What happens next
The expense appears in the expense ledger and can be included in reports.

## Tips or reminders
- Expenses do not change sales totals.
- Keep notes short but clear.

## Common questions or issues
- **The expense needs approval**  
  Wait for a manager or approved person to review it.
