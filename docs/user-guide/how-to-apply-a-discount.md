# How to Apply a Discount

## What this feature does
This reduces the sale total when the customer is eligible for a discount.

## When to use it
Use this for allowed discounts such as senior, PWD, or store-approved discounts.

## Before you begin
- Confirm the customer is eligible.
- A manager may be needed for large or special discounts.

## Steps
1. Add the customer items to the cart.
2. Open the discount option during checkout.
3. Choose the discount type.
4. Enter any required customer details.
5. Review the new total.
6. Continue with payment.

## What happens next
The discounted amount appears in the total and on the receipt when applicable.

## Tips or reminders
- Check customer ID when your store policy requires it.
- Ask a manager if POSard requests approval.

## Common questions or issues
- **The discount is blocked**  
  The amount may be above the allowed limit or your role may need manager approval.
