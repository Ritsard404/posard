# How to Handle Cash In and Cash Out

## What this feature does
This records cash added to or removed from the drawer during a session.

## When to use it
Use this when adding starting cash, withdrawing petty cash, or recording drawer movement.

## Before you begin
- A cashier session must be open.
- Know the correct amount and reason.
- Be online so the manager approval can be verified securely.

## Steps
1. Open **Point of Sale**.
2. Choose the cash action, such as withdraw cash.
3. Enter the amount.
4. For a withdrawal, enter a short operational reason such as petty cash, bank deposit, or supplier payment.
5. Ask a manager to approve if POSard requests it.
6. Confirm the action.

## What happens next
The drawer record updates and appears in session reports.

## Tips or reminders
- Record cash movement immediately.
- A withdrawal reason is required and is retained in the audit trail.
- Do not use cash withdrawal for customer debt payments or sales.

## Common questions or issues
- **The amount is blocked**  
  A manager may need to approve the cash movement.
- **The Withdraw button is disabled**  
  Enter a positive amount, a withdrawal reason, and the manager PIN when the terminal requires approval.
- **Cash withdrawal says online approval is required**  
  Reconnect the terminal, then retry. Cash withdrawal is not queued offline because the manager approval must be verified online.
