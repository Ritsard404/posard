# How to Start a Cashier Session

## What this feature does
This opens your cashier shift and records the starting cash in the drawer.

## When to use it
Use this before making sales for your shift.

## Before you begin
- Select the correct terminal.
- Count the starting cash in the drawer.

## Steps
1. Open **Point of Sale**.
2. Select **Open Session** if the system asks.
3. Enter the starting cash amount.
4. Confirm the session.

## What happens next
The POS screen becomes ready for sales. Your shift activity is connected to your account.

## Tips or reminders
- Enter the real cash amount in the drawer.
- Do not share your cashier account with another person.

## Common questions or issues
- **I cannot start a session**  
  The terminal may already have an open session or your account may not have access.
