# How to Set Up Company Information

## What this feature does
This stores the business details used across POSard.

## When to use it
Use this when setting up a new company or updating official business information.

## Before you begin
- You need manager or admin access.
- Prepare the business name, address, contact details, and receipt details.

## Steps
1. Open **Company** or **Business Info**.
2. Review the current business details.
3. Update the needed fields.
4. Save the changes.
5. Check receipts and terminal details if the change affects printing.

## What happens next
POSard uses the updated business information where it applies.

## Tips or reminders
- Keep receipt details accurate.
- Ask an admin if company access is missing.

## Common questions or issues
- **I cannot edit the company**  
  Your role may not allow company settings changes.
