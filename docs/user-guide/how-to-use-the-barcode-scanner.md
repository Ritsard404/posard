# How to Use the Barcode Scanner

## What this feature does
This helps cashiers add items to the cart by scanning a product barcode.

## When to use it
Use this when products have saved barcodes and you want faster checkout.

## Before you begin
- The product must have a barcode saved.
- The scanner or camera must be ready.

## Steps
1. Open **Point of Sale**.
2. Open the barcode scanner option.
3. Scan the product barcode.
4. Check that the correct item was added to the cart.
5. Continue scanning or proceed to checkout.

## What happens next
The matching product is added to the cart.

## Tips or reminders
- Keep barcode labels clear and readable.
- Check the cart if the scanner beeps but no item appears.

## Common questions or issues
- **The barcode is not found**  
  Ask a manager to add or correct the barcode in the product record.
