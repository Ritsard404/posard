# How to Use Variants, Units, Serials, and Bundles

## What this feature does
Variants, units, serials, and bundles help stores track exact sellable options, unit conversions, warranty items, and grouped products.

## When to use it
Use it for apparel sizes and colors, hardware units, electronics serial numbers, warranty items, and kits or bundles.

## Before you begin
- Add the base product first.
- Decide whether stock is tracked by piece, pack, case, meter, kilo, liter, serial, or bundle component.
- Prepare barcode, serial, IMEI, warranty, and component information.

## Steps
1. Open **Business Fit** to confirm the business type and setup path.
2. Open **Products & Inventory**.
3. Use the product tracking mode that matches the item.
4. Add variant, unit, serial, or bundle details where needed.
5. Review inventory health and product reports after selling.

## What happens next
POSard keeps the product linked to the right tracking records so managers can review exact stock and warranty exposure.

## Tips or reminders
- Keep pharmacy batch or expiry lots separate from serial-number tracking.
- Use base units consistently so reports stay readable.
- Use bundles only when staff understand whether component stock should be consumed.

## Common questions or issues
- **Should a service be a variant?**  
  No. Use a service or non-stock product mode for service fees.
- **Should a medicine batch be a serial item?**  
  No. Use batch and expiry receiving for pharmacy stock.
