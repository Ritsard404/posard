# How to Choose a Payment Method

## What this feature does
This records how the customer paid for the sale and can show manual payment QR/details for configured reference payment methods.

## When to use it
Use this during checkout after the cart total is correct.

## Before you begin
- Confirm the final amount with the customer.
- For non-cash payments, prepare the payment reference if needed.
- If using a QR or bank transfer, ask the customer to pay outside POSard first.

## Steps
1. Open checkout.
2. Choose cash, card, or e-payment.
3. If QR/details appear, let the customer scan or copy the details.
4. For cash, enter the amount received.
5. For card, QR, bank transfer, or e-payment, enter the amount and payment reference.
6. Review the total paid and change.

## What happens next
POSard checks if the payment is enough to complete the sale. Reports still use the selected payment method label.

## Tips or reminders
- Do not leave payment references blank if your store requires them.
- A visible QR does not mean POSard confirmed payment. The cashier must verify payment outside POSard and enter the reference.
- Check the change amount before finishing.

## Common questions or issues
- **The sale cannot continue**  
  The payment may be incomplete or missing a required reference.
