# How to Use the Dashboard

## What this feature does
The dashboard shows important store activity in one place.

## When to use it
Use it at the start of the day, during operations, or before reviewing reports.

## Before you begin
- Sign in to POSard.
- Use a manager account for the full business view.

## Steps
1. Open **Dashboard**.
2. Review the summary cards.
3. Check **Live Store Status** for open drawers, approvals, sync queue, printer setup, kitchen queue, low stock, and customer display health.
4. Review **Variance Investigation** after drawers close to compare expected cash, counted cash, and possible reasons for over or short amounts.
5. Review **Restock Assistant** when it appears to see suggested reorder quantities and stock days left.
6. Check alerts that need attention.
7. Open the suggested page if a card shows a problem.
8. Use reports for deeper details.

## What happens next
You get a quick view of sales, sessions, stock, debt, subscriptions, or other items that need attention.
Managers also get a compact live status check, drawer variance explanations, and reorder suggestions for tracked products that may run out.

## Tips or reminders
- The dashboard is a quick view, not a replacement for reports.
- Review warning cards before the end of the day.
- A warning in Live Store Status means the related area should be checked before the rush period or shift closing.
- Variance Investigation helps explain a drawer difference, but managers should still confirm the cash count and review receipts.
- Restock recommendations are based on recent sales pace and current stock. Confirm the shelf count before ordering.

## Common questions or issues
- **Numbers look different from a report**  
  Check the date range and report type.
