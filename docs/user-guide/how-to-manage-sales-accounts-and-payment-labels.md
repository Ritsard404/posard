# How to Manage Sales Accounts and Payment Labels

## What this feature does
This controls the card and e-payment labels cashiers choose during checkout.

## When to use it
Use this when your store adds or changes payment methods.

## Before you begin
- You need manager access.
- Decide the exact payment names cashiers should see.

## Steps
1. Open **Sales Accounts**.
2. Add a new account or edit an existing one.
3. Enter the display name and account label.
4. Save the account.
5. Check checkout to confirm the payment option is clear.

## What happens next
Cashiers can choose the updated payment label during checkout.

## Tips or reminders
- Use names staff recognize, such as GCash, Maya, Card, or Bank Transfer.
- Avoid duplicate payment names.

## Common questions or issues
- **A payment label is confusing**  
  Rename it before cashiers use it in more sales.
