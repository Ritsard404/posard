# How to Manage Sales Accounts and Payment Labels

## What this feature does
This controls the card and e-payment labels cashiers choose during checkout. Managers can also add QR images and customer-facing account details for manual payments such as Maya, GCash, bank transfer, or card terminal references.

## When to use it
Use this when your store adds or changes payment methods.

## Before you begin
- You need manager access.
- Decide the exact payment names cashiers should see.
- Prepare the merchant QR image and public payment details customers may scan or copy.

## Steps
1. Open **Sales Accounts**.
2. Add a new account or edit an existing one.
3. Enter the display name and account label.
4. Upload or replace the QR image if customers should scan one.
5. Enter the provider, account holder, account or mobile number, and short customer instructions.
6. Turn on **Show these details during checkout** when the details are ready for cashiers and customers.
7. Save the account.
8. Check checkout to confirm the payment option and QR/details are clear.

## What happens next
Cashiers can choose the updated payment label during checkout. If display details are enabled, POSard shows the QR/details while the cashier still records the payment reference.

## Tips or reminders
- Use names staff recognize, such as GCash, Maya, Card, or Bank Transfer.
- Avoid duplicate payment names.
- POSard only displays your payment instructions. It does not confirm, settle, reverse, or verify the money movement.
- Do not store customer card numbers, bank passwords, OTPs, or gateway secrets in these fields.

## Common questions or issues
- **A payment label is confusing**  
  Rename it before cashiers use it in more sales.
- **The QR/details do not appear in checkout**
  Edit the payment method and make sure **Show these details during checkout** is turned on.
