# How to Manage Free Mode and Donations

## What this feature does
This lets admins keep POSard free, turn paid subscription enforcement on later, and show optional donation details with one or more QR, bank, or wallet accounts.

## When to use it
Use this when changing platform pricing behavior or updating the optional donation QR, bank, wallet, or support details shown to users.

## Before you begin
- You need admin access.
- Confirm whether POSard should stay free or enforce paid terminal subscriptions.
- Prepare the donation QR or bank details for each account you want to show.

## Steps
1. Open **Admin**.
2. Open **System Settings**.
3. Choose **Free mode** or **Paid mode**.
4. If donations are enabled, add one donation account for each bank, wallet, or QR code.
5. Fill in the label, provider, account holder, account details, QR or bank info image, and any public note for each account.
6. Turn off **Show this account** for any account you want to keep saved but hidden.
7. Save settings.

## What happens next
Free mode keeps POS checkout available without active subscription charges. Paid mode makes subscription status affect terminal access again.

## Tips or reminders
- Donations are optional and do not automatically unlock features.
- Enabling paid mode can block stores with inactive terminal subscriptions.
- Keep private notes out of public donation messages.
- Use separate donation accounts when you accept donations through multiple banks or wallets.

## Common questions or issues
- **Cashiers are blocked after paid mode is enabled**  
  Check the company subscription page and reactivate the affected terminal subscription.
