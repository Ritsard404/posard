# How to Use Supplier and Purchase Order Pages

## What this feature does
Supplier and purchase order pages help managers track what the store plans to buy and receive.
For pharmacy stock, receiving can also record batch number, expiry date, and shelf location so POSard can prioritize the oldest safe batch first.

## When to use it
Use this when ordering products from suppliers or checking receiving history.

## Before you begin
- You need manager access.
- Supplier and product details should be ready.
- Prepare the delivered quantity, batch number, expiry date, and shelf location when the supplier delivers stock.

## Steps
1. Open **Suppliers** to check or add supplier details.
2. Open **Purchase Orders** to review orders.
3. Check the supplier, status, expected date, and total.
4. Use receiving records when items arrive.
5. Enter the received quantity, batch number, expiry date, and shelf location.
6. Review stock movement after receiving.
7. Open **Inventory Health** to check the Batch & Expiry Monitor for expired and near-expiry lots.

## What happens next
Purchasing and receiving records become easier to audit and compare with inventory. Received batches are tracked for expiry, and POSard uses FEFO logic during checkout when batch stock exists.

## Tips or reminders
- Do not update product cost carelessly.
- Check delivered quantity against ordered quantity.
- Do not receive expired stock unless a manager intentionally needs it recorded for audit.
- Keep batch numbers exactly as printed on the supplier invoice or product packaging.

## Common questions or issues
- **The order is only partly received**  
  Keep the order open until the remaining items arrive or the manager cancels the balance.
- **POS blocks a product after receiving**
  Check Inventory Health. The remaining tracked batches may be expired or blocked, so receive a non-expired batch before selling.
