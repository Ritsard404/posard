# How to Use Business Fit Workflows

## What this feature does
Business Fit helps managers choose a setup path for the store and track work that should stay visible before final checkout.

## When to use it
Use it for service bookings, repair jobs, wholesale orders, restaurant open tickets, and prescription checks.

## Before you begin
- Choose the company business type in company settings.
- Review terminal overrides if one counter works differently from the rest of the store.
- Add customers, products, service items, and terminals first when possible.

## Steps
1. Open **Business Fit**.
2. Review the business fit matrix.
3. Create the needed workflow record, such as a service booking or repair job.
4. Fill in customer, terminal, staff, date, amount, reference, and notes when they apply.
5. Save the record.
6. Use POS checkout when the work is ready to become a final sale.

## What happens next
The record appears in the matching queue so staff can follow up without using a retail-only workaround.

## Tips or reminders
- Use service items for fees that should not reduce stock.
- Use repair jobs when parts, deposits, warranty, or pickup status must stay visible.
- Use wholesale orders when a customer needs terms, a quote, or delivery notes before invoicing.
- Use restaurant open tickets when a transaction must be held before settlement.

## Common questions or issues
- **I cannot see customers or products in the form**  
  Add the customer or product first, then return to Business Fit.
- **Should I use POS or Business Fit?**  
  Use POS for immediate checkout. Use Business Fit when work must be tracked before final checkout.
