# How to Use the Customer Display

## What this feature does
The customer display shows the customer their cart, total, payment, or completed sale.

## When to use it
Use this when your store has a second screen for customers.

## Before you begin
- The terminal must have customer display enabled.
- Open the customer display page on the second device or screen.

## Steps
1. Open the customer display link for the terminal.
2. Keep it visible to the customer.
3. Use POS normally on the cashier screen.
4. Check that cart changes appear on the customer display.
5. Leave the display open during checkout.

## What happens next
The customer sees sale progress without touching the cashier screen.

## Tips or reminders
- Use the correct terminal display link.
- Do not show manager or report pages on the customer screen.

## Common questions or issues
- **The display is not updating**  
  Check the connection and make sure both devices use the same terminal.
